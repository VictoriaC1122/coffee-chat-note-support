COFFEE CHAT NOTE

Give every conversation a next step.

Job hunting, changing roles or exploring an unfamiliar industry: keep preparation, conversations and follow-through in one notebook.

01

Arrive with a direction

Bring together the person’s background and what you hope to learn. Prepare thoughtful questions to make your time together count.

02

Remember what matters

Connect advice, insights and your reflections to the person and conversation. Search and revisit them when you need them.

03

Keep the connection going

Turn a thank-you, a useful resource or your next check-in into a follow-up. Add a date and an optional reminder.

GETTING STARTED

Start with one name.

On first launch with an empty notebook, choose your language or Follow System, then open the four-step tutorial. Basic notes, people, follow-ups and complete backups are free. No account is needed.

  1. 01

    Add someone you want to know

    Add a person in People. A name is enough to save; fill in the company, role and how you met whenever you like.

  2. 02

    Prepare your next Coffee Chat

    Create a Coffee Chat, choose its participants and add questions in Preparation. Afterward, return to capture notes and reflections.

  3. 03

    Turn an insight into a small action

    Create a follow-up, link it to a person or conversation and set a date. Try a thank-you or an article to share. Enable a reminder only if you want one.

  4. 04

    Keep a copy of your notebook

    Open Settings → Backup and Restore. Export a complete JSON backup and save it somewhere you trust. Import that file on a new device to restore it.

Replay anytime in the app’s Settings → Getting started. You can also change your language and Light or Dark appearance in Settings.

A little help, when you need it.

How do I back up or move my notebook?

Open Settings → Backup and Restore to export a complete JSON backup, including records, relationships and photos. Save it in a trusted place. To move devices, transfer that file and import it on the new device. Preview the restore choices carefully; exports may contain private information.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check notification permission in iOS Settings, enable the reminder on the pending task, and check its date and pause setting. Date-only reminders occur at 9:00 AM in the task’s selected time zone. The nearest 60 future reminders are scheduled and refreshed when you open or edit the app. Focus modes and system notification settings can affect delivery.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the same Apple Account used for the purchase, then open Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases. An active subscription is required. Connect to the App Store if the status cannot be verified. Backup files do not transfer Pro access.

How do I cancel, and what happens to my notes?

Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Canceling stops the next renewal; Pro stays available until the paid period ends. Free notes, existing template answers, and complete backup and restore remain available afterward. Apple handles refund requests.
